BirdCull - find bird photos you probably want to delete, look through them, and free up space.
See also BirdCull_Guide.pdf (picture guide).

FIRST TIME ONLY
  1. Double-click install.bat and wait until it says Done (needs internet, about 8 GB of space).
  2. Use the "BirdCull" icon that appears on the Desktop from now on.

EVERY TIME
  1. Open BirdCull. A browser page opens. Keep the black window open while you use it.
  2. Step 1: choose your photo folder, press Start scan. A big library takes hours - leave the laptop plugged in.
     You can close it and press Start again later; it continues where it stopped.
  3. Step 2: press "Find photos to remove", then look through the photos it suggests.
     Press Keep on any you want to save. NOTHING is deleted yet.
  4. Step 3: choose "Move to Recycle Bin" (safe) or "Delete for good" (frees space now, cannot be undone).

SAFE BY DESIGN
  - It only suggests clear cases: no bird in the photo, a clearly blurry bird, or extra frames of a long burst
    (the sharpest 4 are always kept).
  - Photos with people, other animals, or edits (.xmp/.dng) are never suggested.
  - A record of everything removed is saved in the BirdCull folder in your user folder (removed_photos_log.csv).

PLEASE: there is no backup. Copy your favourite photos to a USB drive or the cloud before the big clean-up.
